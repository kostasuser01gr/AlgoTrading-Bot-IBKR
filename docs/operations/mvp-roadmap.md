# MVP Roadmap

## MVP 1

- Research-only operator.
- Approved source ingestion.
- Multi-model thesis generation.
- Risk-aware recommendation cards.

## MVP 2

- Backtesting workspace and strategy lab.
- Quant worker integration.
- Reproducible backtest manifests.

## MVP 3

- Paper trading mode.
- Execution staging.
- Reconciliation and alerting.

## MVP 4

- Controlled live mode.
- Human approval gate.
- Broker connectivity and kill switch drills.

## MVP 5

- Regime-aware adaptive model weighting.
- Champion and challenger routing.
- Drift detection and rollback-safe reweighting.
